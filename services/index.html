How our water damage services are organized

Water damage is easiest to understand by where the water came from. A burst pipe, a failed appliance, a storm and a flooded basement all leave wet materials, but the first steps and the risks differ. The groups below follow that logic, so you can find the page that matches what you see.

If you are not sure which page fits, call our 24/7 line and describe it. The whole-house overview, including the first 24 to 72 hours, price factors and insurance basics, is on our home page. That page covers water damage restoration in Dumont in general, and the pages below go deeper on one cause or one material at a time.

Every service here follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration, which sets the water categories and the drying approach. You can ask any company whether its crews work to it, including ours.
