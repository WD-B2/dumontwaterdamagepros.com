Where East Dumont is, and how we use the name

East Dumont here means the east side of the borough, around Memorial Park. Dumont has no officially named neighborhoods, and some real estate sites label this area Dumont East. We have no evidence that residents use that label, so we describe the area by landmarks and keep the edges approximate.

Memorial Park is on the east side of Dumont, with five ballparks, a tennis court, a basketball court and a pavilion. Lovell J. Honiss School stands on Depew Street in the southeast part of the borough, according to the borough's history page. Camp Merritt, a military camp opened in 1917, was established along the eastern border, and most of its barracks were later torn down or destroyed by fire.

That history is context only. It does not tell us about the plumbing or the basement in your house. If water is spreading in an east side home now, call the 24/7 line at (844) 950-6900.

Residential water damage on the east side

Dumont had 6,572 housing units at the 2020 Census, and that figure covers the whole borough. We do not have a verified count for the east side, and we do not state how many homes here have basements or crawl spaces. Look at your own house, because that decides which problems you can plan for.

What to do before the crew arrives

If it is safe, go through these steps in order:

  1. Stay out of any room where water touches outlets, cords or appliances.
  2. Shut off the water at the main valve only if you can reach it without stepping in water.
  3. Do not use household fans or an ordinary vacuum on flood or sewage water.
  4. Take photographs and video of the damage from a dry spot.
  5. Lift unaffected belongings off wet floors if it is safe to do so.
  6. Call the electric utility or 911 if you see sparks, smell gas or suspect electrical danger.

We cannot tell you whether it is safe to restore power. A professional has to assess electrical, structural and contamination hazards first.

How the drying works

A crew starts by finding where the water went. A moisture meter reads wood and drywall, and a thermal camera can show cool, wet areas behind a surface. Standing water is removed, soaked materials that cannot dry are taken out, and the rest is dried with air movers and dehumidifiers.

EPA guidance says to dry wet materials within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, so the first day matters. We do not claim that all materials can be saved, and no one can promise that mold will never develop. Our water extraction page explains the first stage in more detail.

Insurance documentation

Photos taken before cleanup, a list of damaged items and moisture readings from the drying can help describe what happened. Whether the damage is covered depends on its cause and your policy, so check your policy and ask your insurer. We do not promise approval, payment or any particular outcome.
