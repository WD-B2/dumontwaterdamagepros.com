Seasons and the reservoir: when Oradell homes get wet

Oradell has a reservoir at its core. The Oradell Reservoir Dam on the Hackensack River was built between 1921 and 1923, and the reservoir is also fed by Pascack Brook and the Dwars Kill. The dam reduced flooding in eastern Oradell, according to published histories. That is a real benefit, but not a guarantee.

Published accounts say that during Hurricane Irene in August 2011 the reservoir rose above the top of the dam, which sent water over it and worsened flooding in Oradell and New Milford. What that means for you: a dam lowers the risk, and a major storm can still overwhelm it.

Places and what they tell us

Oradell Memorial Field sits across from Oradell Public School, and the Doug Parcells Athletic Complex is on Ridgewood Avenue. The Atwood-Blauvelt mansion on Kinderkamack Road dates to 1897. A house near these older parts of town may have older plumbing, while newer homes may have newer supply lines.

The former New Milford plant of the Hackensack Water Company on Van Buskirk Island operated until 1990. It is a reminder that this stretch of the river has been a working water source for a long time.

Shops and offices along Kinderkamack Road

Kinderkamack Road, County Route 503, runs through the middle of Oradell and is the main route for local business. A shop or office there can suffer the same causes as a house: a burst supply line, a roof leak, a sump failure or a storm.
