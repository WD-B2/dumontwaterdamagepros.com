What to have ready when you call

A few facts help us give you useful advice in the first minute:

  • The address where the water is and the nearest cross street.
  • The source, if you know it, such as a pipe, a heater, a roof or a drain.
  • Whether the water is clean, gray or from sewage.
  • Whether you have shut off the main water valve.
  • Which rooms and floors are wet.
  • Your insurer's name, if you plan to file a claim.

If anyone is in danger, or you smell gas, see sparks or hear electrical buzzing near water, leave the area and call 911 first.

Before we arrive

If it is safe, shut off the main water valve and turn off power to wet areas at the breaker. Move valuables and electronics to a dry room. Take photos and video of the damage before you move anything else. Our guide on what to keep or toss explains how to sort belongings later.

We cannot promise an arrival time, because it depends on where crews are and what the weather is doing. We can promise that we will tell you what to do while you wait.
