What happens after you call

Speed is the main variable in water damage. EPA guidance says to dry wet materials within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, so the early steps matter most.

  1. 1

    Call and describe

    Say what is wet, where it started and whether it is still running. A crew is arranged and a same-day response is offered when available.

  2. 2

    Stop and extract

    Crews confirm the source is off, then remove standing water with pumps and extraction units.

  3. 3

    Map and dry

    Moisture meters and thermal cameras find hidden water. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure.

  4. 4

    Check and rebuild

    Daily readings show when materials are dry. Then repairs begin, or you hand off to your contractor.

Water categories and what they mean for the job

Professional water damage restoration sorts water into three categories under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard. The category sets the safety steps and the decision on what can be dried and what must be removed.

Water categories at a glance
CategorySource examplesUsual approach
Category 1Supply line, tank leak, rain through a clean roofExtract and dry, many materials can be saved
Category 2Washing machine or dishwasher overflow, sump waterExtract, clean, remove porous materials as needed
Category 3Sewage, river or flood waterContainment, protective gear, remove porous materials. See sewage backup cleanup

Water that is clean at first becomes Category 2 or 3 if it sits, which is another reason to call early.

Water damage restoration in Dumont: the borough and what it means for a loss

Dumont is a Bergen County borough of about two square miles, with 6,572 housing units in the 2020 Census. It was incorporated in 1891 as Schraalenburgh and renamed in 1898. A borough that has been built up for well over a century has houses of many ages, so ask what your plumbing is made of before something fails.

Washington Avenue and Madison Avenue are the main roads, and Knickerbocker Road, County Route 505, runs along the east side. A shop at Washington Commons and a house beside the park face different risks, which is why we ask where you are when you call.

The schools show the range of building ages. Grant and Lincoln schools opened in 1911, Dumont High School in 1932, Honiss School in 1955 and Selzer School in 1960. Building dates vary across the borough, and the cause and the cleanup depend on each building's own materials and plumbing.

What shapes the price of water damage repair in Dumont

No honest company can name a price before someone sees the water. These are the factors that move it:

  • The water category: clean, gray or contaminated water changes the safety steps and what can be saved.
  • The affected area and how many rooms the water reached.
  • Materials involved, such as carpet, hardwood, drywall, plaster or finished basement walls.
  • How long the water sat before drying started.
  • Equipment days and whether repairs follow the drying.

Insurance and water damage, in plain terms

Sudden and accidental water damage, such as a burst pipe, is often covered by homeowners policies. Gradual leaks and neglect often are not, and flood damage usually needs separate flood coverage. We never promise a claim will be approved. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and we will document the loss with photos and moisture readings.
