Who we are and what we do

Dumont Water Damage Pros is an emergency water damage line for homeowners, landlords and businesses in Dumont, New Jersey, and the nearby towns of Haworth, Cresskill, New Milford, Demarest and Oradell. We answer calls around the clock and send licensed and insured restoration professionals to the job.

Our work starts when water is where it should not be. That includes a burst pipe, a failed water heater, a leaking roof, a backed-up drain, a flooded basement or a slow leak that has been wetting a wall for weeks. We stop what can be stopped, remove the water, dry the structure and explain what comes next.

We are a call-only service. There is no form to fill in and no booking page. You call one number, describe what happened and talk to a person about what to do first.

How a call works

Every call follows the same short path:

  • You describe the source of the water, how long it has been running and which rooms are wet.
  • We tell you what to do right now, such as shutting off the main valve or the power to a wet area.
  • We explain the scope and the price before work starts, so nothing about the job is a surprise.
  • Crews extract water, set drying equipment and take moisture readings.
  • We explain what remains, such as removing wet material or rebuilding, and what to bring to your insurer.

How we think about the work

Water damage is a race against moisture. Wet drywall, flooring and framing can begin to grow mold if they stay wet, and EPA guidance says to dry wet materials within 24 to 48 hours. That is why we treat every call as urgent and why the first hour matters more than the first week.

We follow the published industry standards for the work. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers water damage restoration and S520 covers mold remediation. We name them as the standards the industry uses, and we encourage you to ask any company, including ours, whether its crews work to them.

We also try to be plain about what we do not know. We do not diagnose health problems from mold, because that is a question for a physician. We do not promise that a claim will be approved, because only your insurer decides coverage. We do not guarantee that mold will never return, because no one can promise that while moisture is possible.
